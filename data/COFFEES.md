## How to read this catalog

- **Roaster tasting notes** are marketing or product-page descriptors, not independently confirmed flavors.
- **ROASTER / coffee-specific** indicates the published recipe accompanied that coffee; **ROASTER / house guide** applies to a brewer rather than that exact bean.
- **EXPERIMENTAL** means an editorial recommendation, not a roaster-published or brew-tested recipe.
- **Missing information** is stated as such, not silently invented. The Barn’s AeroPress house guide lacks an official dose; its V60 house guide omits temperature.
- **Grind descriptors** shown here are the source recipe’s general terms. The separate grinder document has X-Ultra dial settings and adjustment advice.
- **Water mass** means water added, not liquid yield. All masses are grams, and temperatures are Celsius.

##### Hario Switch immersion

**Recipe provenance:** EXPERIMENTAL · editorial trial recipe (`editorial_suggestion`)  
**Dose:** 20 g · **Water added:** 320 g · **Ratio:** 16.00:1  
**Temperature:** 92°C · **Source grind description:** medium-coarse

4. Open valve; drain through paper.

**Notes:** This 20 g / 320 g full-immersion dose is for Hario Switch **03 / 360 mL** only. For Switch **02 / 200 mL**, use **12 g coffee / 180 g water** at the same temperature and steep time. **Recipe source:** Editorial proposal — no roaster recipe URL

**Steps**

1. Standard orientation, paper filter rinsed; put AeroPress on a sturdy cup.
2. 0:00 add 225 g water to 15 g ground coffee; give 2–3 gentle stirs.
3. Insert plunger slightly to stop dripping.
